# AIJ novelty attack

Audit date: 2026-09-08. Purpose: identify the defensible contribution, not establish priority. No new experimental run is authorized by this report.

## Verdict

Representation search, predicate invention, theory repair, prospective testing, software ablation and harness-aware evaluation all have substantial predecessors. Even the warning that parser/scaffold choices can distort both positive and negative capability judgments is explicitly present in current agent-evaluation work. The manuscript must not present any of these as its isolated invention.

The defensible contribution is an implemented, inspectable coupling: **a prospective executable assay separating structural representational escape from retrospective fit, linked to field-level provenance, replay interventions and an inventory of supplied executable knowledge.** Its empirical value is the different diagnoses this coupling yields for the same headline success/failure scores. The scope is bounded synthetic hybrid systems, not a universal discovery theory.

## Search and evidence method

Primary publisher, proceedings, institutional author-copy and arXiv sources were searched using theory revision, belief revision, constructive induction, predicate invention, ontology revision, representation search, abduction, meta-interpretive learning, scientific discovery, hybrid provenance, scaffold attribution, tool/model attribution and failure attribution, followed by exact-title retrieval. Recent AIJ open-world papers supplied an additional backward-citation route. The prior NMI audit was used only as a lead list: its phrases “incumbent oracle,” “uniquely combines,” and “outcome-before-commitment” are not carried forward. The last phrase reverses the actual protocol and is incorrect.

In the matrix, **NR** means the particular test was not established in the inspected material, not proof that no related test exists anywhere in that work. **NA** means the model/scaffold question is not the work's target. Prospective evidence is distinguished from retrospective construction of a pre-conclusion task. Publication status is not inferred from an arXiv upload alone.

## Strongest attacks and required manuscript response

### “Constructive induction already did this”

Correct about representation change. Our contribution is an evaluation contract: a candidate must satisfy observational adequacy, declared structural non-membership, prediction/action commitment before outcome reveal, improvement against a selected observationally optimal comparator and held-out falsification. The contract connects these checks to content provenance. We do not offer a more expressive representation learner than MIL or a new general theory of induction.

### “Your J1 is syntax”

J1 measures canonical structural non-membership, not functional inexpressibility. The candidate is additionally required to discriminate prospectively from one selected finite-set comparator. These are separate predicates; conjunction does not turn them into a universal semantic expressivity theorem. Formal definitions must make this limitation visible before results.

### “The scaffold supplied the answer classes”

Yes: the motif-to-basis inventory is part of the studied system. Search organization can be evaluated under that inventory while representation invention remains a stronger unestablished claim. The held-out triadic family is held out from search-side family construction, not from the realizer. Report the graph source and executable-law source separately.

### “Component audit is obvious from the code”

Code inspection identifies an overwrite but does not by itself supply the whole reusable evidential record. The proposed procedure joins five objects: prospective commitment, field-level provenance, executable replay, component intervention and supplied-knowledge inventory. Together they distinguish (i) output rejected before execution, (ii) model graph accepted but law supplied externally, (iii) model expression entering prediction, and (iv) a fully scaffold-authored evaluated theory. The historical self-composition, grammar-constrained and C3 paths instantiate (i), (ii) and (iv). The separate supplied-representation DeepSeek sensitivity instantiates (iii): its model-authored expression and action are evaluated without overwrite, with 3/40 world successes. This demonstrates occasional use of a supplied representation, not independent representation invention or a model-necessity effect estimate.

Replaying many rows is not an independent conceptual contribution. The number of rows supports implementation consistency only. The transferable output is the contract/schema and the resulting claim distinctions, with exact replay as its validation mechanism in this bounded setting.

### “Modern harness evaluation already says both success and failure are confounded”

Correct. Cite Zhu et al., Zhang et al. and TraceElephant directly. The additional question here is who supplied the executable explanatory content of a prospectively validated representation change, not whether a model's benchmark ranking changes across wrappers. Do not claim this study measures the relative importance of model versus harness across general tasks.

### “This is too synthetic for a Regular Paper”

Exact ground truth supports auditable membership, commitments, comparator selection and replay; it does not confer external validity. The paper's value depends on whether readers can reuse the evaluation record and distinguish otherwise conflated claims. If that method is not concretely specified, more prose or more internal replay counts will not rescue the contribution. No new domain or model experiment is proposed here.

## Required editorial changes

- Add direct classical representation-repair and modern harness-attribution comparisons, including their stronger capabilities where applicable.
- Replace “oracle” with finite comparator set and selected observation-optimal comparator throughout AIJ prose.
- Remove “first,” “unique combination,” and blanket claims that previous systems lack component analysis.
- Include a portable evidence-record specification and a claim/evidence table; distinguish an audit recommendation from a demonstrated result.
- Describe replay as post-hoc and inference-free; no general model-necessity conclusion.
- Treat newer preprints as preprints unless their current proceedings status is separately verified.

## Residual uncertainty

A supplementary grey-literature search located [Can LLM-Centered Systems “Jump”?](https://github.com/Egonso/can-llm-centered-systems-jump), v0.2.0 (29 July 2026; accessed 8 September 2026). Its README describes an AI-generated, unreviewed discussion object with no accepted scholarly author, a conditional computability argument for a scaffolded system, and a self-administered synthetic reconstruction with freeze and archive checks. This is relevant to system-boundary arguments but is not evidence of reliable model capability or an independent empirical baseline. The present audit inspected its stated scope, not every proof or executable file. It further counsels against blanket priority or structural-impossibility claims; it is recorded here without making it a foundation of the manuscript.

This is a focused primary-source literature audit, not proof of absence over all AI. Repository code of every predecessor was not independently reexecuted or exhaustively inspected. NR cells must never be strengthened to categorical absence in the manuscript. Model Discovery Agent v4 is especially close and supplies more explicit component separation than the prior NMI audit credited. No publication-critical contradiction in the frozen experimental artifacts was found by this literature audit; the response is narrower positioning, not additional experiments.
