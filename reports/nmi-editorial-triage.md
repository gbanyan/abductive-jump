# Simulated NMI editorial triage

## Editorial question

Would an NMI editor send this Article to external review on significance, breadth, novelty, evidence and journal fit?

## Strengths

- Timely problem at the intersection of LLM reasoning, scientific agents and benchmark validity.
- The paper defines a construct that current novelty/performance benchmarks usually conflate.
- Preregistered comparisons, exact world-level truth, prospective intervention freeze and full replay provide unusually strong internal validity.
- The two-stage design answers the obvious “prepackaged operator menu” objection rather than hiding it.
- The proposal–reasoning factorial offers a mechanistic result rather than another leaderboard.
- Transparent negative controls and zero exclusions align with NMI's current emphasis on reproducibility and agent-component disclosure.

## Desk-rejection risks

1. **Synthetic benchmark breadth:** editors may view this as an engineered demonstration without sufficient real-world AI significance.
2. **Perfect performance:** C3 can look tautologically aligned with the generator unless the genericity and outcome-blindness constraints are explained visually.
3. **Single small model:** using only Phi-4 limits contemporary relevance, even though rerunning models is outside this publication phase.
4. **Title/claim mismatch:** “discovery” could read broader than the evidence; the abstract must keep the synthetic qualifier.
5. **Priority ambiguity:** representation invention is established historically; novelty must be the prospective assay and factorial isolation.
6. **Submission incompleteness:** no author metadata, archival DOI or finished figures currently exists.

## Likely editor decision on the present package

**Not yet sendable, but scientifically plausible for review after packaging.** The scientific narrative is coherent enough for an Article. The largest non-administrative risk is external validity, not numerical integrity. A cover letter should foreground measurement and mechanism and explicitly acknowledge why exact synthetic worlds are necessary.

## Changes most likely to improve triage without new experiments

- Make Figure 1 exceptionally clear about the prospective criterion and human-specified components.
- Use the measurement-oriented title, not a claim that models “jump.”
- Put CJ5 and the held-out caveat in the abstract.
- Add a compact design table that shows matching and identifies unequal reference conditions.
- Provide an immutable repository DOI and polished source-data-backed figures.
- Ensure the first page states the broad relevance: distinguishing hypothesis-space expansion from more search in agent evaluations.
