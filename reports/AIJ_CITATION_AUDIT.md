# AIJ citation integrity audit

Initial access/review date: 2026-09-08; section-review update: 2026-09-10. Reference numbers below are those in the AIJ manuscript. Publisher records, author manuscripts, author-hosted repositories and original-text reproductions were used; search-engine dates were not treated as publication dates. This is a bibliographic and claim-support audit, not independent reproduction of the cited experiments. Primary-paper methods for the nearest predecessors were inspected in the novelty audit; a book's general conceptual role does not imply a complete rereading of that book.

## Corrections and remaining checks

Full titles restored for Stahl, AI Feynman, AI Scientist, Pearl and Peters et al.; ABC authors/pages and Timber pages completed; UniACE advanced to its current v3 title; PiEvo status labeled using the consulted author version. POPPER's correct proceedings URL retained. All 45 entries are cited in the main text; supplementary numeric references refer to the same list.

No unsupported “first” wording remains. No AI-generated reference summary is treated as evidence that a predecessor lacks a feature. A current journal-specific bibliography formatting check remains part of upload compliance; PiEvo's official page range can be added if a proceedings record becomes available. This does not change the substantive citation.
