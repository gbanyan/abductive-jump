# NMI novelty attack

Date: 2 September 2026

## Strongest version of the rejection

“This is a carefully engineered synthetic benchmark around ideas already present in conceptual change, predicate invention, program synthesis and LLM-guided evolutionary search. The system succeeds because the authors supplied the mutation ontology and evaluators. It therefore does not show autonomous representation invention, scientific discovery, or a general limitation of frozen LLMs.”

That rejection is substantially correct if the paper uses any of those broad claims. The publishable contribution is the measurement design and the causal separation it enables.

## Vulnerabilities that remain after comparison

1. **Designer prior:** generic mutation operators and typed slots encode meta-knowledge about plausible representation changes.
2. **Syntheticity:** procedural worlds offer exact truth and oracles but not ecological scientific importance.
3. **Benchmark ease:** C3 reaches 100%; the benchmark discriminates conditions, not frontier capability.
4. **Model dependence:** the primary claim is about the tested frozen model/interface, not all LLMs.
5. **Held-out adjacency:** the held-out arity-three relation extends an already represented binary-relation family and is not a wholly alien ontology.
6. **Distance bound:** depth four is the registered operator distance within this mutation system, not global minimal conceptual distance.
7. **Attribution:** success belongs to the composite scaffold. It does not show that the LLM alone invented the representation.
8. **Priority uncertainty:** fragmented terminology prevents a categorical “first.”

## Required manuscript language

Use: “bounded representation-level escape,” “within the registered mutation system,” “for the tested frozen model and synthetic families,” “to our knowledge,” and “scaffold-level capability.”

Avoid: “genuine creativity,” “autonomous discovery,” “LLMs can/cannot jump,” “minimal edit distance” without the registered-system qualifier, “zero risk,” and “generalizes to novel science.”

## Verdict

**Novel enough for submission only as a benchmark-and-mechanism paper.** The novelty is not the existence of representation change; it is the prospective operationalization and factorial evidence that representation proposal, rather than reasoning alone or brute-force incumbent search, is the bottleneck in these registered worlds.
