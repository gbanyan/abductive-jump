# Reviewer #2 report

## Recommendation

**Major revision before submission.** The two-phase preregistered design is unusually auditable and the proposal–reasoning separation is potentially valuable. The central empirical results appear internally consistent. However, the paper will fail if framed as evidence of general abduction or autonomous representation invention. Its strongest form is a benchmark-and-mechanism Article.

## Major comments

1. **Operator answer encoding.** AJ5's nine-member menu plainly contains family-aligned high-level changes. The manuscript now treats AJ5 as assay validation and uses CJ5 for the stronger claim. This is acceptable only if Figure 3 shows exactly what information remained encoded in generic strata.
2. **Perfect C3 performance.** A 500/500 jump score is more likely to trigger concern about benchmark–search co-design than admiration. Report coverage across candidate positions, primitive paths and families, and state that this is condition discrimination rather than frontier difficulty.
3. **Held-out strength.** `triadic_relation_reification` is structurally held out but conceptually adjacent to binary relation reification. “Held-out structural family” is acceptable; “novel concept” is not.
4. **Self-proposal baseline.** C_self tests Phi-4 plus one constrained serialization and budget. It cannot support a general model-capability claim. Provide valid/malformed plan rates and representative failure categories in Extended Data.
5. **Unequal computation semantics.** Equal calls/candidate slots/interventions do not make all proposal computations equivalent. C1 and C5 must remain reference conditions, and the paper must report attempted and valid operations and actual tokens.
6. **External validity.** Exact synthetic oracles make the construct measurable but remove central difficulties of real science. The Discussion appropriately treats domain transfer as future work; do not imply the held-out generator solves this.
7. **Novelty priority.** Conceptual change, constructive induction and predicate invention precede the work. Retain “to our knowledge” and define the claimed first as the exact prospective benchmark combination.
8. **Human contribution.** The authors designed families, representations, operators, strata, fitter and verifier. A workflow schematic must mark each human-specified component.
