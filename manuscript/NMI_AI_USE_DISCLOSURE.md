# Proposed AI-use disclosure

The original experiments used a frozen `microsoft/phi-4` model as a registered experimental component. A separately frozen completion-budget sensitivity retained that model revision and 4-bit runtime while changing only the completion cap from 700 to 2,048 tokens. The minimal targeted sensitivity extension additionally used the same Phi-4 revision at 8-bit precision and the locally served `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp` checkpoint under matched, native-reasoning and supplied-representation conditions. One separately frozen grammar-constrained condition used the same DeepSeek checkpoint with independent reasoning and answer stages, explicit parser syntax and no semantic feedback. Prompts, revisions, runtimes, decoding settings, budgets and outputs are described in the Methods and archived artifacts. A post-hoc component audit established that the original C3 scientific fields and verdicts were deterministic and did not require model output; a second provenance audit established that DeepSeek supplied graph-edit topology while the scaffold supplied motif realization, fit, ranking and intervention. A separately frozen, inference-free counterfactual replay then quantified dependence on the motif realizer and role/action-aware field binding without generating new model output. These limitations are reported in the main text.

OpenAI conversational and coding models were used during study development to discuss methodological alternatives, stress-test interpretations, inspect artifacts, implement and orchestrate separately commit-frozen sensitivity and counterfactual replay extensions, recompute inference-free audits, verify literature metadata and journal guidance, and assist manuscript drafting and consistency checks. They did not generate or modify historical confirmatory source data, change frozen evaluation rules or determine authorship. All protocols, code changes, statistical outputs, citations and interpretations were reviewed and approved by the human authors, who retain full responsibility for originality, accuracy, interpretation and integrity.

Before submission, the authors will replace the generic product description with the exact conversational and coding products used and their date ranges, after checking the complete research record.

All authors must review and approve this disclosure before submission.
