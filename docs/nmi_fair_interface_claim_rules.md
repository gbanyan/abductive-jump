# Fair-interface interpretation rules

This is an internal author audit, not Supplementary Information. It records the outcome-neutral
interpretation boundaries fixed before the 96-world fair-interface condition completed.

## Claims supported regardless of outcome

- The assay measures bounded, prospective hypothesis-space expansion under a frozen language and
  J0-J5 evaluator.
- End-to-end system success does not identify whether the decisive representation came from the
  model, deterministic search, the evaluator or their interaction.
- The C3 model-free replay attributes its successful scientific fields to deterministic scaffolding.
- Historical autonomous results were confounded by a mismatched and budget-limited serialization
  interface.
- The fair condition removes the identified outer-schema, operator-key and reasoning-to-answer
  allocation confounds without providing semantic or outcome feedback.

## Outcome-contingent interpretations

- If fair-interface JSR improves, report that the registered interface underestimated capability
  on this checkpoint, panel, DSL and candidate budget.
- If many plans execute but fail J1 or J2, report a proposal-quality limitation under the tested
  operator language and procedural-world distribution.
- If plans reach J1-J2 but fail J3-J5, report that structural novelty and observational adequacy did
  not translate into prospective utility under the frozen evaluator.
- If fair-interface JSR remains zero, report the exact latest gate reached. Do not convert that
  result into a model-independent conceptual incapacity.
- If fair-interface performance approaches deterministic C3, report that this checkpoint and
  reasoning regime recover much of the proposal function on the fixed sensitivity panel; do not
  treat n=96 as a replacement confirmation.

## Prohibited extrapolations

- Do not claim that language models generally can or cannot expand hypothesis spaces.
- Do not describe the paper as an anti-hype, anti-cheating or debunking study.
- Do not attribute errors to other systems such as FunSearch or The AI Scientist without auditing
  those implementations.
- Do not relabel synthetic variables as a scientific domain to imply external validity.
- Do not treat worlds sampled from one generator as independent structural replications.
- Do not describe grammar-constrained output as constrained scientific reasoning; only
  serialization form, operation names and static argument types are guaranteed.
