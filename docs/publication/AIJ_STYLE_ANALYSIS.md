# AIJ structural style analysis

Audited 2026-09-08. Purposive sample of 12 original-research articles published in AIJ during 2022–2025, weighted toward representation, reasoning, search and open-world systems. This is not a representative bibliometric sample. Original-research status is clear from contributions and journal records; portal-level “Regular Paper” labels were not exposed consistently, so exact category metadata remains unverified. No editorial, survey or position paper is counted.

## Cross-paper synthesis

Titles name a formal object, task, algorithm or precise problem. Colons are common enough to be available, not obligatory. None of these structures requires a broad-impact news headline. Abstracts often carry definitions, method and technical result together; the sample does not justify a universal 150-word constraint. The AIJ draft will target 200–250 words editorially while the current guide remains unverified.

Introductions typically occupy roughly two to three inspected pages, sometimes incorporating model definitions or contribution lists. Pagination is version-dependent and must not become an artificial target. Related Work may be early, late, embedded or combined with background. For this manuscript, an early independent section is warranted because constructive induction and theory revision define the gap.

Formal depth tracks the claim: theorem papers develop proofs; system papers specify interfaces, architectures and evaluation. Our manuscript needs coherent definitions and an executable decision procedure, not decorative theorems or invented guarantees. Results should answer scientific questions; operational amendments belong in a reproducibility appendix.

Discussion/limitations range from a closing combined section to independent sections. We will use separate Discussion and Limitations because attribution and generality are different questions. Figures and tables vary substantially; neither the sample nor accessible official scope pages supports retaining NMI's display-item limit. Keep only figures that distinguish assay stages, search levels, provenance, interface attrition or realizer dependence.

## Adopted storyboard constraints

1. Put classical representation limitations before modern LLM motivation.
2. State three contributions: executable prospective criterion, search-space decomposition, provenance/replay procedure.
3. Define representation language, finite executable comparator set and selected observational optimum separately.
4. Give a worked commitment-before-reveal example and a reusable claim/evidence table.
5. Treat deterministic realization and interface repair as central results, not caveats at the end.
6. Preserve technical completeness in a supplement without recounting every scheduling failure.

Read-only PDF inspection used text extraction plus representative rendered pages (published planning front matter and open-world architecture evaluation). No paper text or artwork is copied. Remaining limitation: the sample includes author/pre-proof versions and one partial current full-text access; it supports structural editorial choices, not a claim to have verified all final production details.
