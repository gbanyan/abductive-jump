## Research question and argument

How can a system prospectively demonstrate escape from an incumbent structural representation, and which components supplied the evaluated explanatory content? Classical AI establishes why representations constrain search. Current discovery and agent-evaluation systems establish that representations can change and scaffolds matter. The remaining target here is an executable evidence contract joining these questions.
